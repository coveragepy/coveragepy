- Feature: the :meth:`.Coverage.get_plugins` method returns the currently
  registered plug-ins, including disabled ones. The new
  :meth:`.CoveragePlugin.is_enabled` method reports their current enabled state.
  This provides a supported way for plug-in tests to inspect their registration,
  as requested in `issue 920`_.

.. _issue 920: https://github.com/coveragepy/coveragepy/issues/920
