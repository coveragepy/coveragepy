- Feature: two new methods for plug-ins to use in their test suites, as
  requested in `issue 920`_.  The :meth:`.Coverage.get_plugins` method returns
  the currently registered plug-ins, including disabled ones. The
  :meth:`.CoveragePlugin.is_enabled` method reports their current enabled
  state.

.. _issue 920: https://github.com/coveragepy/coveragepy/issues/920
